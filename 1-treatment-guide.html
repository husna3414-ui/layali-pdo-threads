What is a PDO thread lift?

PDO stands for polydioxanone — a soft, medical-grade material that has been used safely in surgery (for dissolvable stitches) for decades. In an aesthetic thread lift, very fine PDO threads are introduced beneath the skin using a thin needle or blunt cannula.

They work in two complementary ways:

Lifting threads (cog / barbed)

  • Have tiny barbs or "cogs" along their length
  • These grip the underlying tissue
  • The thread is drawn upwards to reposition sagging tissue
  • Gives an immediate, visible lift

Regenerating threads (mono / smooth / screw)

  • Smooth "mono" threads or twisted "screw" threads
  • Placed in a mesh to stimulate collagen
  • Improve skin firmness, texture & quality
  • Result builds gradually over the following weeks

Both types are fully absorbable. The threads themselves dissolve over roughly 6 months, broken down naturally by the body — but the lift and the new collagen they trigger last considerably longer, because your own tissue has been repositioned and strengthened.

How it works over time

The clever part

  • Straight away — lifting threads physically reposition the tissue, so you see an immediate lift.
  • Weeks 2–12 — your body responds to the threads by forming new collagen and fine supportive scaffolding around them.
  • Around 6 months — the PDO threads themselves have fully dissolved, but the collagen and the repositioned tissue remain.
  • The result — firmer, better-supported, lifted-looking skin that outlasts the threads.

Is a PDO thread lift right for me?

You may be a good candidate if you have

  • Mild to moderate skin laxity
  • Early jowls or a softening jawline
  • Cheeks that have begun to descend
  • A wish to lift without surgery
  • Good general health
  • Realistic expectations

It may NOT be suitable if you

  • Have severe sagging better suited to a surgical facelift
  • Are pregnant or breastfeeding
  • Have an active infection or skin condition in the area
  • Have an autoimmune condition, bleeding disorder or a history of keloid/poor scarring
  • Have very thin, fragile skin
  • Expect a permanent or surgical-level result
Important: a PDO thread lift is not a substitute for a surgical facelift. It is ideal for mild to moderate laxity — for significant sagging, surgery may give a better result, and we will always tell you honestly. A consultation determines suitability, and treatment may be declined if it is not safe or appropriate.

Your results — realistic expectations

A thread lift gives you two results in one: an immediate lift from the threads themselves, and a gradual improvement in firmness and skin quality as new collagen forms.

When you'll see it

  • Immediate lift visible on the day (though initially with some swelling)
  • Swelling settles over 1–2 weeks to reveal the true lift
  • Collagen-led firmness continues to build over 6–12 weeks

How long it lasts

  • Typically around 9–18 months
  • Threads dissolve by ~6 months; the lift & collagen outlast them
  • Depends on age, skin quality, thread type & lifestyle
  • Top-up or maintenance sessions can extend results

Results vary from person to person and cannot be guaranteed. Some clients benefit from a combination of lifting and regenerating threads, or from more than one session for the best result.

What happens during treatment?

Consultation & planning

We assess your skin, facial structure and goals, mark your lift vectors and agree the thread type and number with you.

Preparation

The area is cleansed with antiseptic and local anaesthetic is used to keep you comfortable.

Placement

The threads are gently introduced beneath the skin through tiny entry points using a fine needle or cannula, then lifted into position. Most clients describe pressure or mild pulling rather than pain.

Aftercare

You go home the same day. We give you full written aftercare — the first 1–2 weeks are important for protecting your result.

How many sessions will I need?

Many clients achieve a lovely result from a single session. Depending on the degree of laxity, the area and your goals, we may recommend combining lifting and regenerating threads, or a second session to build the result. Maintenance sessions can be planned as the effect gradually softens over time.

Downtime & common side effects

A PDO thread lift is minimally invasive, so some downtime is normal. Most people feel presentable within a few days, though bruising and swelling can take up to 1–2 weeks to settle fully.

Common & temporary

  • Swelling
  • Bruising
  • Tenderness & soreness
  • A tightness or pulling sensation (especially with movement)
  • Mild dimpling or puckering at entry points that settles
  • Small bumps or areas of firmness while healing

The pulling sensation is normal and eases as healing progresses — it often feels most noticeable when you first open your mouth wide or smile broadly, which is why we ask you to limit big facial movements at first.

Important risks

A PDO thread lift is generally well tolerated, but it is a minimally invasive procedure and all treatments carry risk. We explain everything fully at your consultation before you decide.

Common & temporary

  • Swelling & bruising
  • Tenderness
  • Tightness / pulling sensation
  • Mild dimpling or puckering
  • Small bumps at entry points
  • Temporary asymmetry from swelling

Less common / more serious

  • Thread migration or extrusion (a thread moving, or a thread end poking through the skin)
  • Infection
  • Persistent asymmetry
  • Persistent dimpling or puckering
  • Visible or palpable threads under the skin
  • Nerve irritation or, rarely, nerve injury (altered sensation or muscle weakness)
  • Granuloma (a firm inflammatory lump)
  • Prolonged pain or discomfort
  • Vascular injury (a thread affecting a blood vessel)
  • Unsatisfactory result / need for further treatment or thread removal
Signs to contact us urgently: a thread tip poking through the skin, spreading redness, heat, pus or fever, severe or worsening pain, or marked new asymmetry. Contact Layali Clinic on 07787 708200. If you feel seriously unwell, seek urgent medical care or call 999.

When PDO threads are not advised

Please tell us if you

  • Are pregnant or breastfeeding
  • Have an active infection, cold sore or skin condition in the area
  • Have an autoimmune or connective-tissue condition
  • Have a bleeding disorder or take blood-thinning medication
  • Have a history of keloid or poor scarring
  • Have very thin or fragile skin
  • Have had recent filler in the same area (may need caution or timing adjustment)
  • Have a known allergy to PDO / polydioxanone

This is not a full list — your consultation and medical history form ensure treatment is safe for you.

Alternatives to consider

A thread lift is one of several ways to address laxity. At your consultation we'll help you choose what genuinely suits your face — sometimes that's threads, sometimes it's something else, and sometimes it's a combination.

Non-surgical options

  • Dermal filler — to restore lost volume & support
  • HIFU / Titan X — energy-based skin tightening
  • Endolaser — laser lifting & tightening beneath the skin
  • Combining threads with the above for a fuller result

Other options

  • Surgical facelift — for significant laxity
  • No treatment — always a valid choice

Frequently asked questions

Will it look natural?

Yes. The aim is to lift and support your own tissue and reawaken your collagen — so you look fresher and firmer rather than "done".

Does it hurt?

Local anaesthetic keeps you comfortable. Most clients feel pressure or mild pulling rather than pain during placement, with some soreness afterwards.

Is it the same as a facelift?

No. A thread lift is a non-surgical option for mild to moderate laxity. It won't replicate a surgical facelift for significant sagging — we'll always advise you honestly.

How long do the results last?

Typically around 9–18 months. The threads dissolve by about 6 months, but the lift and the new collagen they stimulate last longer.

Do the threads dissolve completely?

Yes — PDO is fully absorbable and is broken down naturally by your body over roughly 6 months, leaving nothing permanent behind.

Is there much downtime?

Most clients are back to normal quickly, but swelling and bruising can take 1–2 weeks to settle. Avoiding wide mouth movements and big facial expressions early on protects your result.

Will I need more than one session?

Many clients achieve a great result from one session; some benefit from combining thread types or a second session depending on their goals.

What does it cost?

How do I know if I'm suitable?

The easiest way is a quick, no-obligation consultation (or a WhatsApp message with a couple of photos). We'll honestly tell you whether threads are right for you, or if another treatment would suit you better.
