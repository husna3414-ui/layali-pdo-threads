Intended benefits

A PDO thread lift is a non-surgical treatment that works in two ways: absorbable threads lift and reposition sagging tissue immediately, and as they dissolve they stimulate your own collagen to firm and support the area over time. Please make sure you understand the following:

  • The aim is a subtle, natural lift and improved firmness. It cannot replace a surgical facelift and cannot correct significant skin laxity or heavy sagging.
  • It best suits mild to moderate laxity — the level of change is modest compared with surgery.
  • You will usually see an immediate lift on the day, and the collagen-led firmness builds gradually over the following weeks.
  • The final settled result is generally assessed at around 2–4 weeks, once any swelling has resolved.
  • Results typically last around 9–18 months and vary from person to person.
  • You may need a repeat session for your desired result, and maintenance treatments over time to keep the effect.
  • No specific result can be guaranteed.

Alternatives

Reasonable alternatives to a PDO thread lift have been explained to me, including:

  • No treatment at all, and accepting the current appearance
  • Delaying treatment to consider it further or seek more information
  • Dermal filler to restore volume and support
  • HIFU or Titan X (energy-based skin tightening)
  • Endolaser (fibre-laser skin tightening)
  • A surgical facelift or neck lift for significant laxity
  • Skincare and lifestyle measures (e.g. sun protection, not smoking, medical-grade skincare)
  • Referral to a specialist where appropriate
  • Seeking a second opinion

Risks & complications

No procedure is entirely risk-free. A PDO thread lift is minimally invasive, and the following risks may occur, grouped by how often they happen. Unforeseen risks may also occur that are not listed here.

Common

  • Swelling, bruising & redness
  • Tenderness & soreness
  • Tightness or a pulling sensation with movement
  • Mild dimpling or puckering at entry points (usually settles)
  • Small bumps or firmness while healing
  • Temporary asymmetry from swelling

Less common

  • Persistent dimpling, puckering or asymmetry
  • Thread migration or extrusion — a thread moving, or a thread end working its way to the surface
  • Visible or palpable threads under the skin
  • Granuloma — a firm inflammatory lump
  • Prolonged pain or discomfort
  • Unsatisfactory result / need for further treatment or thread removal

Rare but serious

  • Infection — which may need antibiotics or thread removal
  • Nerve injury — altered sensation, or (rarely) temporary muscle weakness
  • Vascular injury — a thread affecting a blood vessel
  • Significant or lasting scarring

Emergency warning signs — act quickly

Contact Layali Clinic immediately on 07787 708200 if you develop:

  • Severe or rapidly increasing pain
  • White, grey, blue, purple or black skin
  • Blistering or skin breakdown
  • Rapidly increasing swelling
  • Spreading redness or heat
  • Pus or discharge
  • A thread poking through the skin, or a visible thread end
  • Fever or feeling severely unwell

Call 999 or go to A&E immediately if you develop:

  • Breathing or swallowing difficulty
  • Facial or throat swelling
  • Chest pain
  • Visual disturbance or loss of vision
  • Weakness, confusion, collapse or other neurological symptoms

Acting quickly matters — some complications are time-critical, so please do not wait. Do not attempt to remove a thread yourself. Out of hours, if you cannot reach the clinic, call NHS 111, or 999/attend A&E for anything severe or rapidly worsening.

Tell any other healthcare professional (A&E, GP, 111) the treatment you had, the product, the date and the injection/treatment site(s), and show them this consent so they can help you quickly.
