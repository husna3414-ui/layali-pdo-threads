What to expect — expectations

A PDO thread lift is a non-surgical lifting and collagen-stimulating treatment. Please make sure you understand the following before you proceed:

  • It is not a surgical facelift and will not achieve the same degree of lift — it suits mild to moderate laxity.
  • The threads are fully absorbable and dissolve over roughly 6 months; the lift and collagen last longer, typically around 9–18 months.
  • You'll usually see an immediate lift, but swelling can mask or exaggerate it at first — the true result appears once swelling settles (around 2 weeks).
  • Collagen-led firmness builds gradually over the following weeks.
  • More than one session, or a combination of thread types, may be recommended.
  • Results vary between individuals and are not guaranteed. Maintenance is needed over time to keep the effect.

Risks & complications

No procedure is entirely risk-free. A PDO thread lift is minimally invasive, and the following risks may occur. There may also be unforeseen risks not listed here:

Common / temporary

  • Swelling, bruising & redness
  • Tenderness & soreness
  • Tightness or a pulling sensation with movement
  • Mild dimpling or puckering at entry points (usually settles)
  • Small bumps or firmness while healing
  • Temporary asymmetry from swelling

Less common / more serious

  • Thread migration or extrusion — a thread moving, or a thread end working its way to the surface
  • Infection — which may need antibiotics or thread removal
  • Persistent dimpling, puckering or asymmetry
  • Visible or palpable threads under the skin
  • Nerve irritation or injury — altered sensation, or (rarely) temporary muscle weakness
  • Granuloma (a firm inflammatory lump)
  • Prolonged pain or discomfort
  • Vascular injury (a thread affecting a blood vessel)
  • Unsatisfactory result / need for further treatment or thread removal

Infection & emergency warning

Although rare, infection, a thread working its way out (extrusion) or a vascular problem require prompt attention. Please contact us straight away if you notice:

  • A thread poking through the skin or a visible thread end
  • Spreading redness, heat, pus or discharge, or a fever
  • Severe or worsening pain rather than gradual improvement
  • Marked or new asymmetry
  • Skin turning white, grey, blue or mottled, or unusually cold
  • Any breathing difficulty or feeling very unwell

I understand that if any of these occur I must contact Layali Clinic immediately (07787 708200), must not attempt to remove a thread myself, and, if severe or feeling seriously unwell, must call 999 / attend A&E.
